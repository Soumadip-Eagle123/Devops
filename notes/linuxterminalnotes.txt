cat file.txt | tr a-z A-Z > upper.txt 
  Step by step explanation->
   cat file.txt reads the contents of the file
   | is a pipe, that passes the contents into a buffer
   tr a-z A-Z translates all the small letters to caps inside the buffer
   > upper.txt writes the content in the buffer in upper.txt
